# Domain Context — ux-improve

## Glossary

- Care tips — the AI-generated recommendations for one plant (watering, light, soil, temperature, tips). NOT advice guaranteed to be correct; their state is ready, generating or failed. Generation starts automatically when a plant is added, so a plant with no tips and no generation under way is treated as failed. A plant with stored tips is ready even if a later regeneration failed.
- Collection — all plants that belong to one gardener. NOT the plant catalogue or search results.
- Collection status — the short summary above the dashboard table: how many plants the collection holds and how many care tips are ready, generating or failed. NOT a to-do list or reminders.
- Dashboard — the plant table page the gardener opens daily to see what is in the collection and its current state. NOT the welcome page.
- Gardener — a person who keeps a personal plant collection in the product. NOT an administrator; there are no other roles.
- Light needs — a short note on the dashboard saying how much light a plant needs, taken from its care tips. NOT the full care tips; those live on the plant page.
- Location — where the plant is kept, inside or outside. NOT the plant's growth conditions such as light.
- Plant — one entry in a collection with a name, type, location and optional care tips. NOT a plant species record shared between gardeners.
- Plant page — the page for a single plant showing all its details and full care tips. NOT a row on the dashboard.
- Watering — a short note on the dashboard saying how often a plant needs water, taken from its care tips. NOT a watering log or a reminder.

## Out of scope

- Urgent actions, reminders and notifications · future functionality, watering cannot be computed reliably.
- Onboarding and welcome page · separate feature.
